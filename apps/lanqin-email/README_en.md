## Introduction

**LanQin Email** is a self-hosted email system with modern webmail and administration features.

## Features

- **Modern Webmail**: Supports folders, search, labels, stars, rich text composition, attachments, and scheduled sending.
- **Mail Service Stack**: The all-in-one container integrates Postfix, Dovecot, and Rspamd with SMTP, IMAP, POP3, DKIM, and spam filtering.
- **Administration**: Manage multiple domains, mailboxes, aliases, permission groups, templates, audits, and DNS checks.
